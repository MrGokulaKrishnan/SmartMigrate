What this prototype shows

  • Glass is for chrome. The navbar, dock, and panel are glass. The desktop stream stays solid so blur never sits on a 60 fps video.
  • Colour behind glass. Glass reads as glass only over colour. The violet light in the stage background is doing that job.
  • Copy follows real states. Use the buttons on the right to step through connecting, connected, reconnecting, and failed.
  • Honest security. Until TLS ships, the UI says "Not encrypted yet" rather than showing a shield.

Glass recipe

Fill rgba(255,255,255,.16 → .05), border rgba(255,255,255,.16), blur 24px, saturate 180%, top specular edge, and a soft drop shadow. Pill radius 999px.
