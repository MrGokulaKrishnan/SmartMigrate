01

Nothing is silently granted

View, mouse, keyboard, files, clipboard, and audio are independently requested and host-approved.

02

Pairing has an expiry

Future QR and numeric pairing are designed as short-lived, single-use approvals—not permanent access links.

03

Unfinished features stay unavailable

The foundation does not simulate streaming, control, cryptography, or transfer capability before native adapters and security tests exist.
